How To Measure

1. Underbust (Band)

Wrap a soft measuring tape snugly around your ribcage, directly under your bust. Keep the tape parallel to the ground.

2. Overbust (Bust)

Measure across the fullest part of your bust, holding the tape loosely so it doesn't compress your skin.

3. Waist & Hips

Measure your natural waistline (narrowest part above your navel) and hips (widest part around your glutes).
